Data: blockchain.info (BTC price), alternative.me (Fear & Greed Index). This composite blends 50-day MA distance, 5-day-smoothed Fear & Greed, daily RSI(14), Bollinger %B(20), and MACD histogram (12/26/9) into one 0-100 score. It's a practical technicals-based tool, not a separately back-tested edge like the long-term dashboard — earlier testing found these short-lookback indicators tend to confirm trend rather than call reversals (see the repo README for that finding in full). Five-tier zones (Extreme Buy/Buy/Neutral/Sell/Extreme Sell), by request — the plain 3-zone version spends ~72% of days in "neutral" even though the composite legitimately swings from single digits to the high 90s; checked directly, real local price peaks score a median of only ~64 (already inside "neutral"), and the literal October 2025 all-time high scored 69.5, under the old sell threshold of 70. Extreme Buy/Extreme Sell reuse the same ≤20 / ≥80 rarity cutoffs the long-term dashboard uses (roughly the rarest 5% on each side for this composite too), added purely for display granularity on the genuinely rare blow-off/capitulation days. Sell threshold moved from 70 to 60 (buy stays at 30), by request, after noticing sell almost never triggered while buy did — real, not perception: at 30/70 the composite spent 15.5% of days at/below 30 but only 12.1% at/above 70 (full history), and far worse recently (last 12 months: 20.5% buy vs. just 3.3% sell). The cause traces to the two most heavily-weighted inputs (50-day MA distance, Fear & Greed) sitting below-50 medians and crossing the low side roughly twice as often as the high side. Moving sell to 60 flips the imbalance the right way (28.0% of days at/above 60 vs. 15.5% at/below 30, full history) rather than splitting the difference with a symmetric 60/40, and better matches real turning points (60 catches 57% of real local price peaks vs. 31% at 70). The cost: confirmed signal frequency rises to ~15-16/year from the original ~9/year design target. Not separately walk-forward validated as an improvement — the justification is distributional/descriptive accuracy against real peaks and troughs, not a forward-return claim; see the repo README for the full numbers. Use it for sizing partial trims/adds, not as a standalone reason to trade. Not financial advice — run st_current_status.py for a live check.
